Preface

Everyone knows that tsumego is the way to build up your reading power. Tsumego, however, are rather hard to get into. The easy ones tend to be shapes you feel you have seen somewhere before, and with the hard ones you cannot tell the head from the tail - there is nothing to take hold of, and the real trouble is that you may not even feel any desire to solve them.

This book collects “Let’s Enjoy Docking,” an enormously popular series from Monthly Go Study. Every problem has the connection of stones as its objective, so the shapes are fresher than those of tsumego, the focus is easier to narrow down, and they are easier to think about. On top of that, you build up your tesuji and life-and-death skills at the same time, so this is truly a book that makes you stronger while you enjoy yourself.

The problems are divided into four levels of difficulty for convenience, but naturally an intermediate player may be baffled by an elementary

3

problem, and an elementary player may solve an intermediate problem with ease. Difficulty varies from person to person. What matters is whether you have met that tesuji before. If you have that memory, the right line comes to you easily and your reading naturally follows through.

So that you can master the tesuji more effectively, the problems are grouped to some extent by similar types of tesuji. Please do apply them in your own games and turn that into a dramatic improvement.

I recently happened to have the chance to travel abroad together with Muraoka Shigeyuki 9-dan, who was responsible for creating the problems in this book. He looked back fondly on the work, saying it had been quite a memorable job.
