Preface

To get stronger at Go, it is important to learn correct shapes with your body. Once you can spot the correct answer the moment you see a problem, you will be able to respond when a similar shape comes up in a real game. Please work through the “At a Glance” series twenty times if you can, and at least five times in any case. Do that, and the basic ideas of Go will become part of you.

During a game, all you have to fight with is what your body has learned. Just as you can climb stairs without paying any attention to it, once you have the knack of playing correctly your stones will head for the vital points of their own accord. In this book I have collected many problems in which playing the first move correctly is enough to defeat your opponent. Take in the thinking behind this book, and learn to discover instinctively the treasures buried on the Go board.

May 2014　Cho Chikun, Twenty-Fifth Honinbo
