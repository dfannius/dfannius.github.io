Preface

Ever since I became a professional, creating life-and-death problems has been my life’s work, and thanks to the support of many people I have been fortunate enough to publish a good number of them in book form.

Most of those problems were quite difficult, however, so they may never have caught the eye of kyu-level players, and especially of beginners.

This time I have put together a collection of easy life-and-death problems, so that kyu-level players - who ought to be enjoying their improvement at Go more than anyone - can grow stronger while having even more fun.

The book is divided into five levels, and it is arranged so that you solve the most important life-and-death techniques over and over. If a problem is beyond you, feel free to look at the answer right away. A technique that puzzles you at first should, through repeated solving, come to mind as easily as drawing breath.

If this book helps you acquire the correct life-and-death techniques and contributes even a little to your improvement, nothing could give me greater pleasure.

November 2014

Kaku Kyushin, 6-dan
